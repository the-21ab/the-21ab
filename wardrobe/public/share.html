Wardrobe Closet

Share your closet

Hand any of these links to another app, a spreadsheet, or a person. They always show what's in the closet right now, no syncing needed.

Links

Google Sheets (a spreadsheet with pictures)

Paste this into cell A1 of an empty sheet. It refreshes on its own about once an hour.

To see the photos, put this in the first empty column on row 2 and fill it down (column F holds the thumbnail link):

=IMAGE(F2)

Excel works too: Data → From Web, then paste the CSV link.

From code

Every item has an id, title, tags, notes, full size image and thumbnail URLs (with width and height), and timestamps. Lists are paged 100 at a time; follow next until it's null.


      

      

    


    

      
Filtering and syncing

      

      
Feeds send an ETag. Send it back as If-None-Match and you get an empty 304 when nothing changed, so polling every few seconds is cheap.
        To mirror the closet somewhere else, poll updated_since for new and edited pieces and /api/deleted?since= for removed ones.

    


    

      
Adding clothes from another app

      
Needs the owner password as a bearer token. The web app shrinks photos and strips location data before uploading; raw API uploads are stored as sent.
